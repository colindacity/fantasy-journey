# Fantasy Reading Quest - Complete Content Structure

## Overview
A gamified fantasy reading guide organized into thematic zones representing major fantasy subgenres, powered by Daniel Greene's expertise, humor, and BookTube personality.

---

**Visual Theme:** Warm, inviting village with coffee shops, bakeries, glowing lanterns, autumn colors

## 📖 BOOK DISCOVERY PATHS

### For New Readers:

#### Path 1: "I've Never Read Fantasy"
1. **Start:** The Hobbit
2. **Next:** Small Gods (if liked humor) OR Mistborn (if wants epic)
3. **Then:** Branch into preferred subgenre
4. **Guide:** Heavy Daniel Greene commentary throughout

#### Path 2: "I Only Know Harry Potter"
1. **Start:** The Name of the Wind
2. **Next:** Mistborn
3. **Then:** Six of Crows OR Ninth House
4. **Guide:** Magic school → hard magic → modern choices

#### Path 3: "I Want Easy Entry Points"
1. **Start:** Legends & Lattes
2. **Next:** The House in the Cerulean Sea
3. **Then:** Choice: Stay cozy OR branch to epic
4. **Guide:** Comfort reads with option to explore

### For Experienced Readers:

#### Path 1: "I've Read Sanderson, What's Next?"
1. **Similar:** James Islington, Brent Weeks
2. **Different:** Fonda Lee, N.K. Jemisin
3. **Challenge:** Malazan Book of the Fallen
4. **Guide:** Based on what they loved about Sanderson

#### Path 2: "I'm Bored of Generic Fantasy"
1. **Start:** Perdido Street Station (the filter)
2. **If loved:** Gideon the Ninth, The City & The City
3. **If hated:** Green Bone Saga, The Poppy War
4. **Guide:** Push boundaries or redirect

#### Path 3: "I Want the BEST"
**Daniel's Masterpiece Recommendations:**
- Green Bone Saga
- Malazan Book of the Fallen
- The Wheel of Time
- The First Law
- The Stormlight Archive
**Guide:** "These are THE ONES"

### Genre Transition Paths:

#### From Romance Novels:
- Kushiel's Dart → A Court of Thorns and Roses → Divine Rivals
